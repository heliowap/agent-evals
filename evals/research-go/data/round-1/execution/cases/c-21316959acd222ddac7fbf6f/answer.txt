## Decision: Not sufficient

**Isolation premise is wrong.** Repeatable Read (RR) in PostgreSQL is Snapshot Isolation: it gives a transaction-start snapshot and, unlike the SQL standard's minimum for RR, also prevents phantom reads — the standard only states minimum protections, and higher guarantees are allowed ([transaction-iso §13.2](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/transaction-iso.html#TRANSACTION-ISO), [#XACT-REPEATABLE-READ](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/transaction-iso.html#XACT-REPEATABLE-READ)). But RR still permits **serialization anomalies** (Table 13.1), and the manual explicitly warns that enforcing business rules at RR "will not work correctly without careful use of explicit locks." A check-then-write cross-row invariant needs **Serializable**, which is RR plus nonblocking monitoring of dangerous read/write conflicts ([#XACT-SERIALIZABLE](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/transaction-iso.html#XACT-SERIALIZABLE); [applevel-consistency §13.4](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/applevel-consistency.html#APPLEVEL-CONSISTENCY)). Stable snapshot ≠ serial execution.

**Recovery premise is wrong.** Statement-level retry cannot work: the snapshot is fixed at the transaction's first query, and the manual says to "abort the current transaction and retry the whole transaction from the beginning." The app must "retry the complete transaction, including all logic that decides which SQL to issue and/or which values to use." PostgreSQL deliberately offers **no automatic retry facility** because it cannot guarantee correctness ([mvcc-serialization-failure-handling §13.5](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/mvcc-serialization-failure-handling.html#MVCC-SERIALIZATION-FAILURE-HANDLING)). The application/framework owns retrying; the retry unit is the whole transaction (savepoints don't reset the snapshot).

**Unique violations:** not an unconditional retry bucket. `23505 unique_violation` (and `23P01 exclusion_violation`) are sometimes effectively serialization failures, but may be persistent errors requiring inspection — retry only after cause analysis. Unconditionally retryable: `40001 serialization_failure`; `40P01 deadlock_detected` is advisable ([errcodes-appendix Table A.1](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/errcodes-appendix.html#ERRCODES-TABLE)).

**Three-attempt promise: no.** "Transaction retry does not guarantee that the retried transaction will complete; multiple retries may be needed. In cases with very high contention... many attempts," and a conflicting prepared transaction may block progress entirely ([§13.5](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/mvcc-serialization-failure-handling.html#MVCC-SERIALIZATION-FAILURE-HANDLING)). UNVERIFIED that any finite-attempt SLA holds; resolve by load-testing the real contention profile.

**Recommend (narrow):** use Serializable for the invariant; retry the whole transaction on 40001/40P01; classify 23505 by cause; document best-effort, not a bound.
