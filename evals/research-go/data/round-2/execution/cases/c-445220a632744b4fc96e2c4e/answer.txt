**Decision: insufficient on three counts.** Retry must be whole-transaction and application-owned; 23505 is conditional; no three-attempt promise is documentable.

**Isolation.** PostgreSQL RR is Snapshot Isolation: one frozen snapshot, and phantoms are also blocked — stronger than the SQL standard's minimum for RR, which "only describes the minimum protections each isolation level must provide" ([transaction-iso](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/transaction-iso.html#XACT-REPEATABLE-READ)). So your phantom premise is factually right but irrelevant: RR still permits serialization anomalies (write skew), and the docs warn "Attempts to enforce business rules by transactions running at this isolation level are not likely to work correctly without careful use of explicit locks" ([applevel-consistency](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/applevel-consistency.html#APPLEVEL-CONSISTENCY)). For a cross-row read-then-update invariant, use SERIALIZABLE for all writes *and* reads that need consistency; it adds nonblocking predicate-lock monitoring and aborts one party with 40001 ([transaction-iso](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/transaction-iso.html#XACT-SERIALIZABLE)).

**Who retries, and what.** The application, the whole transaction. "It is important to retry the complete transaction, including all logic that decides which SQL to issue and/or which values to use. Therefore, PostgreSQL does not offer an automatic retry facility" ([failure-handling](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/mvcc-serialization-failure-handling.html#MVCC-SERIALIZATION-FAILURE-HANDLING)). Your proposal's premise is wrong: PostgreSQL will not replay your earlier decision logic, and the transaction is aborted, so you must ROLLBACK and re-run the logic — statement-level retry is not viable. Also mark read-only work READ ONLY; read-only transactions never have serialization conflicts.

**23505 bucket.** No. Retry 40001 unconditionally, 40P01 as advisable, but for 23505/23P01 "more care is needed … since they might represent persistent error conditions rather than transient failures"; retry only when attributable to a concurrent insert of a read-derived key ([failure-handling](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/mvcc-serialization-failure-handling.html#MVCC-SERIALIZATION-FAILURE-HANDLING)).

**Three-attempt promise.** Not supportable: "multiple retries may be needed," high contention may need "many attempts," and a conflicting prepared transaction may prevent progress entirely ([failure-handling](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/mvcc-serialization-failure-handling.html#MVCC-SERIALIZATION-FAILURE-HANDLING)). UNVERIFIED: any attempt bound for your workload; resolve with a contention load test plus retry/abort metrics.
