## Decision

**Authenticity:** Verify `X-Hub-Signature-256` (HMAC-SHA256, hex digest prefixed `sha256=`) computed over the **raw request body** as UTF-8, using the webhook secret, and compare with a **constant-time** function (`crypto.timingSafeEqual`/`hmac.compare_digest`), never `==` ([validating webhook deliveries](https://docs.github.com/en/webhooks/using-webhooks/validating-webhook-deliveries#validating-webhook-deliveries)). Your pipeline hashes or parses-then-reserializes *before* checking: that breaks verification. **Fix:** buffer the raw bytes, verify first, only then parse. GitHub states payload/headers must not be modified before verification ([troubleshooting](https://docs.github.com/en/webhooks/using-webhooks/validating-webhook-deliveries#troubleshooting)).

**Timing:** Respond 2XX **within 10 seconds**; longer responses are terminated and recorded as failures ([best practices — Respond within 10 seconds](https://docs.github.com/en/webhooks/using-webhooks/best-practices-for-using-webhooks#respond-within-10-seconds)). Your 25-second synchronous job **guarantees failure and duplicate redelivery**. Enqueue and ack immediately; process asynchronously.

**Identifier behavior:** `X-GitHub-Delivery` is the dedup key. GitHub documents that **on requested redelivery the X-GitHub-Delivery header is the same as the original**, and the delivery GUID is constant across redeliveries ([best practices — Use the X-GitHub-Delivery header](https://docs.github.com/en/webhooks/using-webhooks/best-practices-for-using-webhooks#use-the-x-github-delivery-header); [redelivery script](https://docs.github.com/en/webhooks/using-webhooks/automatically-redelivering-failed-deliveries-for-a-repository-webhook#adding-the-script)). Your premise that redelivery yields a new identifier is **incorrect** — dedup on `X-GitHub-Delivery`, not on a per-request ID.

**Retry assumption:** GitHub **does not automatically redeliver** failed deliveries; you must redeliver manually or script it on a schedule ([handling failed deliveries](https://docs.github.com/en/webhooks/using-webhooks/handling-failed-webhook-deliveries#about-webhook-delivery-failures)). Your operations plan's "GitHub retries after an outage" premise is **false**. Build the scheduled poller from GitHub's example ([redelivery script](https://docs.github.com/en/webhooks/using-webhooks/automatically-redelivering-failed-deliveries-for-a-repository-webhook)).

**Minimum changes:** (1) verify raw body, constant-time; (2) ack <10s, process async; (3) dedup on `X-GitHub-Delivery` (accept redeliveries of unseen GUIDs, skip seen); (4) implement your own redelivery poller; (5) HTTPS + optionally IP allowlist ([best practices](https://docs.github.com/en/webhooks/using-webhooks/best-practices-for-using-webhooks#use-https-and-ssl-verification)).

**Exactly-once?** **No.** Docs cover authenticity and GUID stability, not transactional dedup across a crash (crash between side effect and dedup write). UNVERIFIED guarantee; resolve by making dedup-keyed side effects idempotent or transactional — GitHub documents no such mechanism.
