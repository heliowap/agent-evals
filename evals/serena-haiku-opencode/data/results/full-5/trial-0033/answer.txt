The access checks are now enforced in both `src/service.py` and `src/repository.py`, and both check scripts pass.

**What changed**
- **`src/repository.py`**: `find` and `listing` now take `tenant` and `owner`. Every query filters on `tenant=?`, `owner=?`, and `deleted_at IS NULL`, with all values passed as bound parameters. `find` matches on `id` as well. Lists are ordered by `id`. The result shape is still `{id, title, body}`, so no deletion metadata is returned.
- **`src/service.py`**: `get_document` and `list_documents` keep their signatures and pass `tenant` and `owner` through to the repository.
- **`checks/security.py`** (new): covers a live document, a deleted record, a cross-tenant record, a cross-owner record, and a missing ID. All of these return `None` from `get_document`. It also checks that an owner name containing quotes and SQL text is handled as a plain value and that the table is left intact, and that lists are filtered and ordered by id.
