I counted **201** matching requests and wrote that number to `answer.txt`.

I used a short `awk` script that checks the actual status column. A plain text search for "500" would have overcounted, because some paths (`/item/500`) and response sizes (`503`) also contain those digits. Before counting, I checked that all 1,402 lines have the same layout: five fields, a UTC timestamp, and a three-digit status.

The time boundaries are handled as you asked. The log has a 500 at exactly `10:00:00`, which is counted, and another at exactly `11:00:00`, which is not.
