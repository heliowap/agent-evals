# Archived reports (Portuguese originals)

These are the reading copies of the reports written during the study, in Portuguese, passed through the anonymizer. Filenames were translated; contents were not. Their internal links point to the original repository layout and are broken here. The paper in [../../README.md](../../README.md) is the English synthesis. Where a report and the raw data disagree, the paper uses the data.

The curated analyses (`*-analysis.md` and `consolidated-report.md`) are authoritative. Automatic and partial reports are historical snapshots.

| File | Kind | English summary |
|---|---|---|
| `consolidated-report.md` | Curated, consolidated | Three isolated pilots, 216 runs. 36/36 vs 36/36 correct in each configuration. Mean duration +17.1% (Codex/Sol), +23.5% (OpenCode/Sol), +5.8% (OpenCode/Opus, inconclusive). The second-repeat scripting rule added work in `repeat` in all three. Decision: keep the block out of the global AGENTS.md. |
| `agentsmd-codex-pilot-analysis.md` | Curated | Codex CLI 0.162.1, Sol 6.1 xhigh, 72/72. Time +17.1%, output tokens +20.8% after the cumulative-counter correction. Most of the extra time comes from `convert` and `repeat`. |
| `agentsmd-opencode-pilot-r2-analysis.md` | Curated | OpenCode 2.0.25, Sol 6.1 xhigh, 72/72. Time +23.5%, output including reasoning +28.9%, reasoning +195.7%. Exploratory cross-product comparison: OpenCode used 55.3% fewer normalized tokens and 13.1% less time than Codex. |
| `agentsmd-opencode-opus-5-5-medium-analysis.md` | Curated | OpenCode 2.0.25, Opus 5.5 medium via a local OpenAI-compatible proxy, 72/72. Time +5.8%, with an exploratory 95% bootstrap CI of [-7.0%, +24.1%]. The difference is -1.6% when `repeat` is excluded. Independent checks overlapped in 1/3 runs without the block and 0/3 with it. |
