Based on the public primary sources from the PostgreSQL Global Development Group (specifically the official PostgreSQL **Versioning Policy** at `https://www.postgresql.org/support/versioning/`), here are the details regarding PostgreSQL major version 16:

---

### 1. Support Status at the Cutoff (2026-10-07)
* **Status:** **Yes, PostgreSQL 16 is supported.**
* As of October 7, 2026, PostgreSQL 16 remains within its active upstream community support window.

---

### 2. Scheduled Final-Release Date
* **Scheduled Final Release Date:** **November 9, 2028**  
* Following this final scheduled minor release, PostgreSQL 16 will reach end-of-life (EOL) and will no longer receive upstream community bug fixes or security patches.

---

### 3. Length of Support and When the Clock Begins
* **Support Duration:** The PostgreSQL Global Development Group supports each major version for **5 years**.
* **When the Clock Begins:** The 5-year support clock starts on the date of the **initial release** of the major version (for PostgreSQL 16, its initial release was **September 14, 2023**).

---

### 4. Does Installing a Newer 16.x Minor Release Restart the Clock?
* **Decision:** **No, it does not restart the clock.**
* **Explanation:** 
  * The upstream support lifecycle applies to the **major version family** as a whole, measured strictly from the initial release of that major version (16.0). 
  * Minor releases (e.g., 16.1, 16.2, etc.) are regular quarterly updates that deliver bug and security fixes for the major version branch. 
  * While the PostgreSQL community recommends that users always run the current minor release for security and stability, applying a minor release does not extend, defer, or reset the fixed 5-year support window or change the scheduled November 9, 2028 EOL date.

---

### Unresolved Limitations
* The upstream final-release schedule is determined and published by the PostgreSQL Global Development Group. While the policy establishes a standard 5-year window and specifies November 9, 2028 as the final release date for version 16, community release dates may occasionally shift slightly if the release management team adjusts minor release roadmap dates.
