# Validity and limits of round 2

Reading version published at the root of `evals/`. [Original preserved for audit](revisao-de-codigo/round-2/revision-2/methodology-review.md). The links and commands of this version follow the organization by topic.


This review was done **after** sealing the 300 blind decisions in [`revisao-de-codigo/round-2/revision-2/blind-seal.json`](revisao-de-codigo/round-2/revision-2/blind-seal.json). It does not change the cases, prompts, answer key or adjudication files. The 300 responses are valid sessions; this, by itself, does not validate all the targets used to interpret them.

## Targets and scope of the evidence

- **Axios / Standards — target not demonstrable from the prompt alone.** The diff provided shows `return url;` before the conversion and the test with `params=null`, but does not show the condition that executes that return. The external reproduction documented in [`revisao-de-codigo/round-2/revision-2/candidate-research.md`](revisao-de-codigo/round-2/revision-2/candidate-research.md) proves that the test fails at the fixed commit; the reviewers were instructed not to look up other files. The **10/50 hits recorded** measure recognition or inference of a fact that the frozen material is not enough to demonstrate. They remain in the raw record, but **are not a valid measure of comparable coverage across models**. It is not permissible to complete the prompt or replace the target after observing the responses.
- **Requests / Spec — effect conditional on the direct call.** Removing the `/` fallback produces `uri="?foo=bar"` when `build_digest_header()` receives `http://example.com?foo=bar` directly, whereas `PreparedRequest.path_url` returns `/?foo=bar`. Ordinary preparation may insert the slash before authentication calls the helper; it was not demonstrated that a normal request reaches it with that URL without normalization, nor that a server rejects the authentication. The **47/50 hits** concern the change in the helper under that input, not a proven end-to-end regression.

## What the other columns count

- The numbers in [`revisao-de-codigo/round-2/revision-2/adjudication.json`](revisao-de-codigo/round-2/revision-2/adjudication.json) and [`revisao-de-codigo-rodada-2-comparacao.md`](revisao-de-codigo-rodada-2-comparacao.md) preserve the **raw categories of the blind decisions**. A mention classified as `valid_extra` may be the preregistered target **of the other axis** of the same PR or a factual observation about the parser; therefore, 219 mentions are not equivalent to 219 novel or independent defects. Longer responses have more opportunities to mention problems.
- The prompt asked for **fewer than 400 words**. By whitespace splitting, **30/300 responses have 400 or more** (29 exceed 400; maximum: 470); **24/36** responses of the three CPA Opus variants breach this limit. The run did not exclude responses by length. The counts of additional findings were not normalized by length, and adherence to the limit should be considered separately.
- There are **10 claims classified as refuted in the raw record**, but the classification of `b0026` (Axios / Standards) is contested: the response said that no test failure was demonstrable *with the material received*. The guard that would prove that failure was not in it; the adjudication used the external reproduction to refute a compound claim. Do not treat this item as a confirmed false positive of the model. The seal remains intact; this caveat comes after the blind evaluation, not a silent edit of the original judgment.
- The sessions are referenced by ID and metadata verified by the executor. The **300 exports were revalidated** and preserved in [`revisao-de-codigo/round-2/revision-2/session-evidence.json`](revisao-de-codigo/round-2/revision-2/session-evidence.json), with prompt, final text, model, agent, message and part types, completion, tokens and reported cost; the reasoning text was removed. The file allows repeating these checks without depending on the local sessions, but does not preserve the original CLI event stream, the reasoning or the provider's invoice. Each record also stores the SHA-256 hash of the full canonicalized export, without storing it in full.

## Interpretation

This is an **exploratory** comparison between configurations on the same three PRs, not proof that the new wording of the prompts improved the `/code-review` skill. The raw table allows observing duration and responses per target, but **does not support an overall winner nor a six-target score with equal validity**. A new round intending to measure the six targets should freeze an Axios material with the guard visible and decide in advance whether Requests will be evaluated as a helper contract or as a regression reachable through the full flow; this requires new responses, not retroactive reinterpretation of the current ones.
